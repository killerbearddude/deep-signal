## Accepted closeout

P3A–P3C deliver bounded, colony-supported delegated survey, processed-material freight, and survey-instrument servicing. They include finite operational teams, shared typed asset control, actual material transfers, safe program lifecycle handling, and durable reporting.

This operational foundation is sufficient to begin P4A. No P3D is commissioned. The broader parent vision is **not** declared complete.

## Next authorized handoff

**P4A — Observations, analysis, and assessments**, against the baseline above. Its implementation contract is `Deep_Signal_P4A_Implementation_Handoff.md` in this package.

P4A replaces the direct deposit-confidence update with a small observation model, real analyst/laboratory work, immutable assessments and knowledge-limited projections. It reuses existing SurveyTeams rather than creating a second analyst workforce. The same team's field and analysis work is sequenced by actual ownership/location. A separate available team permits real concurrency.

The measurement profiles, detection/category rules, three-team-day job, laboratory rate, input-source model and one-opening information-delivery assumption are **new P4A choices** specified by that handoff. They were not established by the old code or by the audit alone.

## P4 and later boundaries

P4A provides the first evidence/analysis loop. A later P4 development increment must choose a concrete installation and resource-conversion chain, define any necessary freight extension, and exercise both investigated and uninformed development against the same physical world.

**No observation, analyst report, positive indication, or confidence threshold may become permission to build an outpost.** P4A-first delivery is an engineering sequence, not a player prerequisite.

P5 remains optional technical development. P6 remains whole-loop proving, including meaningful alternatives, accounting, longer-session behavior and human play review. Moving forward does not erase the residuals above.
